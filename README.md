# Trips

The app shell for a private travel tracker. **There is nothing of mine in
here**: no booking, no passenger, no reference, no fare. Those live in a
private repository, and the app fetches them in your browser with a
fine-grained token you paste in once, kept on your own device.

So this repository can be public without telling anyone where I am going, and
the app still installs on a phone and opens with no laptop switched on.

Generated by `python flights.py app --publish` in the private repository.
Edits here are overwritten.
